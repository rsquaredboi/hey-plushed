Reference: The original Nancy wordmark — bold hot pink (#ff00d1), chunky serif/script hybrid, confident and playful. All concepts below maintain this DNA.

"Hey" in italic script above, "Plushed" bold below. Full brand lockup for hero sections and marketing.
plushed — Lowercase

Bold serif P in Nancy style. Perfect for favicon, app icon, watermark.

Same logo on charcoal. For dark backgrounds, social headers, merch, packaging.
